Telemetry

Where IB telemetry comes from

For InfiniBand fabrics, port counters, BER, and rate statistics are surfaced through two complementary paths in LLDPq-IB:
Local collection (always on)
ib-monitor.sh polls each switch via SSH every 10 minutes, collecting show interfaces ib counters and ib perfquery / ibqueryerrors output. These feed the Link Errors and Optical pages.
UFM Telemetry (optional)
NVIDIA UFM exposes historical port counters, BER trends, and alarm events via REST. When UFM is configured, the UFM page surfaces alerts and topology cross-checks.
