Fabric Diagnostic (ibdiagnet)

Runs a full-fabric ibdiagnet on the selected target, parses the output into a compact report, and discards the 2-3 GB of raw files (retention keeps the latest archive for download). This is the rich, fabric-wide health source — routing, PHY, rail validation, per-lane PM, cable info, SM/PM state.
"Full run preset" ticks every option (Gregori's reference command).
